## Editorial rules

1. Use US English and US household terminology.
2. Do not translate India-focused posts word-for-word into US articles.
3. Do not claim Mr Glowra is sold or distributed in the US unless that is actually verified.
4. Do not invent prices, certifications, test results, statistics, reviews, credentials, or government approvals.
5. Never recommend mixing cleaning chemicals.
6. Prefer genuinely useful answers over product promotion.
7. Use descriptive headings, concise paragraphs, practical steps, safety notes, and FAQs where useful.
8. Link related articles internally when the site renderer supports them.
9. Keep titles and descriptions natural; avoid keyword stuffing.
10. Keep the site useful to readers who never purchase a Mr Glowra product.
